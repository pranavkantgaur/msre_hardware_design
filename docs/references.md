## How to Access These Reports

Most ORNL technical reports from the 1960s–1970s have been declassified and digitized. They are available through:

1. **OSTI.gov** (U.S. Department of Energy Office of Scientific and Technical Information): https://www.osti.gov  
   Search by report number (e.g., "ORNL-TM-728").

2. **HathiTrust Digital Library**: https://www.hathitrust.org  
   Contains scanned copies of many ORNL technical memos.

3. **ORNL Technical Library**: https://www.ornl.gov/content/ornl-library  
   Contact for hard copies or digitization requests.

4. **Internet Archive**: https://archive.org  
   Search "ORNL molten salt reactor" for scanned documents.
