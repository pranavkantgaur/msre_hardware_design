# Reactor Vessel — Detailed Specifications

**Reference:** ORNL-TM-728, Sections 3.2, 3.3, Appendix A

---

## 1. Vessel Geometry

### Cylindrical Shell
- **Outside diameter:** 1410.0 mm (55.50 in)
- **Inside diameter:** 1384.6 mm (54.50 in)
- **Wall thickness:** 12.7 mm (0.50 in)
- **Shell height (tan-to-tan):** 2134.6 mm (84.0 in)
- **Seam weld:** Single longitudinal seam; double-V groove; full-penetration; 100% radiograph

### Upper Head (Ellipsoidal, Removable)
- **Type:** 2:1 semi-ellipsoidal
- **Outside diameter:** 1410.0 mm (55.50 in)
- **Thickness:** 19.05 mm (0.75 in)
- **Straight flange:** 76.2 mm (3.0 in)
- **Flange OD:** 1460.0 mm (57.5 in)
- **Bolt circle:** 1435.0 mm (56.5 in)
- **Stud holes:** 24 × 38.1 mm (1.5 in) dia, equally spaced
- **Gasket groove:** O-ring groove for nickel ring gasket, 3.2 mm (0.125 in) deep × 6.4 mm (0.25 in) wide

### Lower Head (Ellipsoidal, Fixed)
- **Type:** 2:1 semi-ellipsoidal
- **Outside diameter:** 1410.0 mm (55.50 in)
- **Thickness:** 19.05 mm (0.75 in)
- **Welded to cylindrical shell (permanent)**

### Overall Vessel Height
- Shell tan-to-tan: 2134.6 mm
- Plus lower head crown: ~345.3 mm (head depth ≈ ID/4 = 346 mm)
- Plus upper flange/head: ~420 mm
- **Total assembled height:** ~2900 mm (114 in)

## 2. Pressure and Temperature Ratings

| Condition | Pressure | Temperature |
|-----------|----------|-------------|
| Normal operating | 35–172 kPa gauge (5–25 psig) | 632–654 °C |
| Design (ASME code) | 345 kPa gauge (50 psig) | 704 °C (1300 °F) |
| Pneumatic test | 518 kPa gauge (75 psig) | Ambient (cold) |
| Hydraulic test | NOT APPLICABLE | — (no water testing) |

**Code basis:** Designed to ASME BPVC Section VIII Div. 1 rules for unfired pressure vessels, with Hastelloy-N allowable stress per ASME Sec. II Part D at 704 °C:
- Allowable stress S = ~46 MPa (6,700 psi) at 704 °C for Hastelloy-N
- Required shell thickness at design conditions: t = PD/(2SE) = (0.345 × 1384.6)/(2 × 46 × 1.0) = 5.19 mm → 12.7 mm provided (corrosion allowance and minimum gauge)

## 4. Material Test Requirements

- Chemical analysis per ASTM B575 Table 1 (Hastelloy-N composition limits) on each heat of material.
- Tensile test at room temperature and at 650 °C minimum; one test per heat, per product form.
- Hardness: Rockwell B ≤ 95 (annealed condition); check 3 readings per plate.
- Grain size: ASTM E112, grain size 4–7 (annealed condition).
- Corrosion test (ASTM G28, modified for Hastelloy-N) on sample coupons from each heat.
- No low-carbon (LC) or extra-low-carbon (ELC) modification; standard composition only.

---

## 5. Weld Procedure Requirements

- Process: GTAW (TIG), manual or automatic
- Preheat: None required for Hastelloy-N (minimum ambient temperature 16 °C)
- Interpass temperature: ≤150 °C
- Filler: ERNiMo-1 or ORNL-certified Hastelloy-N matching composition wire
- Shielding gas: 100% Ar, Grade 5 (99.999% purity), flow 15–20 L/min
- Back purge: 100% Ar required on root pass, all full-penetration welds
- Post-weld heat treatment (PWHT): Anneal at 1175 ±14 °C for 30 min/25 mm (1 h/in) of thickness, minimum 30 min; water quench.
- PWHT atmosphere: Ar or He (no air; no oxidizing or carburizing atmosphere)
