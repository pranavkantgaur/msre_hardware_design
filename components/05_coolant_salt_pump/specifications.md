## 6. Comparison to Fuel Salt Pump

| Parameter | Fuel Salt Pump (04) | Coolant Salt Pump (05) | Ratio |
|-----------|--------------------|-----------------------|-------|
| Flow rate | 1200 USgpm | 750 USgpm | 0.625 |
| Motor power | 75 hp | 30 hp | 0.40 |
| Bowl OD | 609.6 mm | 457 mm | 0.75 |
| Shaft length | 1829 mm | 1524 mm | 0.83 |
| Impeller OD | 203 mm | 178 mm | 0.88 |
| Salt density | 2.24 g/cm³ | 1.94 g/cm³ | 0.87 |

The coolant pump operates at lower flow rate and lower power than the fuel pump, with proportionally smaller dimensions. The design philosophy (sump/bowl, cantilevered shaft, no submerged bearings) is identical.

---

## 7. Acceptance Tests

| Test | Acceptance Criteria |
|------|-------------------|
| Hydraulic performance (water test at equivalent density-corrected head) | Flow ≥ 750 gpm at ≥ 60 ft |
| Shaft vibration | ≤2.5 mm/s RMS at bearing |
| Seal leakage | ≤0.01 L/min He to bowl side |
| Motor no-load current | Within 10% of rated |
